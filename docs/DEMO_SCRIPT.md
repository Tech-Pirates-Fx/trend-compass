# 30-Second Video Demonstration Script

**Competition Requirement:** Video demonstration must be strictly **under 30 seconds** in duration.  
**Target Video Duration:** 28 seconds (leaves a 2-second safety buffer).  
**Word Count:** 68 words (approx. 145 words/minute speaking pace).  
**Resolution:** 1080p (1920×1080) at 60 fps, crisp audio.

## Recording & Submission Checklist

- [ ] **Exact Duration:** Audio and video track must end at or before 0:28. Never exceed 0:29.
- [ ] **Visual Clarity:** Record at 1920×1080 resolution. Clear browser cursor (no jitter).
- [ ] **Real Mode Active:** Ensure backend is running with `DATA_ADAPTER=gistemp` (`is_mock=false`, `verified=true`).
- [ ] **Audio Quality:** Clear vocal track, normalized audio levels, optional subtle ambient space-synth background track at -24 dB.
- [ ] **Captions:** Add closed captions (English .srt) on YouTube for accessibility.
- [ ] **Privacy:** YouTube video must be published as **Public** or **Unlisted** (never Private) so judges can view it without requiring login.
